We submit our manuscript entitled

"MAPPING KNOWLEDGE EXCHANGE ACROSS DISCIPLINARY BOUNDARIES IN SCIENCE
EDUCATION: A CITATION-NETWORK AND TOPIC-MODELING ANALYSIS OF 15 JOURNALS,
2019–2026"

for consideration as an Article for peer review in Disciplinary and
Interdisciplinary Science Education Research.

This study addresses the gap between disciplinary and interdisciplinary
science education by computationally mapping knowledge exchange across
15 major journals using citation-network analysis and topic modeling.
We combine bibliometric mapping, brokerage metrics (cross-tag share,
E-I index, Rao-Stirling diversity, betweenness centrality), and topic
modeling to characterize disciplinary silos and interdisciplinary
bridges. Our key finding is that DISER functions as the primary
disciplinary bridge (cross-tag share 0.841, E-I index +0.68), while
JCE remains the most siloed journal (cross-tag share 0.048). We show
that brokerage conclusions are robust across sensitivity analyses
(alternative weighting schemes, direction decomposition, leave-one-journal-out).

This manuscript is appropriate for Disciplinary and Interdisciplinary
Science Education Research because it directly addresses the journal's
scope of advancing scholarship in science education both within and
across disciplinary boundaries. The paper bridges disciplinary science-
education subfields and provides methodological tools for citation-
network and topic-modeling analysis.

We confirm that this manuscript is original, has not been published
previously, and is not under consideration for publication elsewhere.
All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission
to Disciplinary and Interdisciplinary Science Education Research.
